## Withdrawal: German consumer contract

For a distance contract for digital content not supplied on a tangible medium, where a right of withdrawal applies, the withdrawal period is generally 14 days from conclusion of the contract. The period does not begin before the consumer has been properly informed about the right of withdrawal. The withdrawal must clearly express the decision to withdraw and may be communicated without giving a reason, by email or letter to the contact details above. Dispatch within the period is sufficient to meet the deadline. The handling of a valid withdrawal and repayment using the original means of payment must be reviewed against the actual process before publication.

For review of the required statutory form, see the [official model withdrawal form, Annex 2 to Article 246a section 1 EGBGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgbeg/art_253anlage_2.html). The correctly provided statutory model must be included in the final customer materials. The following free-text example is only a practical aid for making a withdrawal declaration; it is not the statutory model and does not replace that obligation:

For online distance contracts, the [electronic withdrawal function under section 356a BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__356a.html), including the acknowledgement of receipt, must be reviewed and implemented where applicable. This draft does not assert that this function or a working acknowledgement delivery service is available. Their final address and placement must be added to the customer notice after implementation.

## Early supply of digital content

For the possible early expiry of the right of withdrawal for paid digital content not supplied on a tangible medium, [section 356(6) BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__356.html) specifies several conditions, including explicit consent to early performance, acknowledgement of the consequences and a contract confirmation under section 312f BGB. Acceptance of the purchase terms alone does not document these separate declarations. The future process and its wording must be reviewed against actual delivery. Until those conditions and their implementation are established, the purchase flow must not assert a blanket loss of the right of withdrawal.

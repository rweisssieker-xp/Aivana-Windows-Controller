4. **Retention implementation:** document operational deletion criteria and implement them for order/claim, entitlement/device (including inactive records), webhook event/deduplication, revoked-payment, support, accounting and infrastructure logs/backups. State applicable statutory accounting periods after accounting review. A continuing license is not a reason to retain every data category indefinitely. The older privacy-data-flows.md 30/90-day proposals are unverified and are not promises in these drafts.
5. **Rate-limit change:** validate the updated minute-bucket hash and two-minute expiry plus request-triggered and five-minute scheduled cleanup in production. Run the explicit legacy migration so prior raw-IP buckets do not remain. Confirm platform request/security logs separately. A database expiry value is not proof of physical deletion or an absolute seven-minute retention guarantee. Do not publish the short-lived-identifier description against the old implementation.
6. **Cookies and rights:** verify the implemented one-year Secure/HttpOnly/SameSite=Lax purchase-claim cookie lifetime/attributes against production; distinguish it from the mobile pairing cookie (30 days). Confirm the legal treatment of necessary storage/access. Confirm seller address, representative/contact, whether a DPO must be identified, the rights-handling process and human activation review, required purchase fields and consequences of withholding them. Confirm whether automatic activation checks require further Article 22 information; do not add a blanket exemption assertion without review.
7. **Final sign-off:** reconcile both language versions with live operations and the final release decision. Publish a stable accessible privacy URL, then verify that URL anonymously before entering it in Partner Center. No publication or portal entry was performed by this draft preparation.

## Delivery and activation

Delivery is intended as a download and a signed, device-bound licence after payment has been confirmed. Purchase and activation require internet access. Alternatively, a device request from an offline PC can be transferred through an internet-connected computer to the activation service and the resulting licence imported to the offline PC. Local offline use is intended after a successful import; periodic online verification is not planned. Customers should keep their download and licence file safely.

Activation uses a product-specific hash of a stable Windows device identifier. A hardware or Windows change may require a new activation. A PC no longer in use can be deactivated in the activation service to free a seat. This is not a promise of tamper-proof device identification: identical clones may share the same identifier. Do not disclose access credentials or purchase recovery secrets. Rights that cannot legally be restricted, including mandatory software rights, remain unaffected.

## Consumers: withdrawal and early supply

For a distance contract for digital content not supplied on a tangible medium, a consumer generally has 14 days from conclusion of the contract to withdraw without giving a reason, where a right of withdrawal applies. The period does not begin before proper withdrawal information has been provided. The consumer can make an unequivocal declaration, including by contacting Aivana at the postal address or email above. Dispatch within the period is sufficient to meet the deadline. Following a valid withdrawal, received payments are generally reimbursed within 14 days of receipt of the withdrawal, using the original payment method unless expressly agreed otherwise without cost to the consumer. The applicable statutory model form and final notice must be made available before ordering and on a durable medium as required. The electronic withdrawal function under [section 356a BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__356a.html) and its acknowledgement must be implemented and reviewed for the actual online purchase.

Immediate supply does not automatically remove the right of withdrawal. For paid digital content not supplied on a tangible medium, section 356(6) BGB specifies several conditions for possible early expiry, including the consumer's explicit consent to early performance, acknowledgement of the consequences, commencement of performance and provision of a contract confirmation under section 312f BGB. Separate, unchecked declarations and their wording must be reviewed against actual delivery. A checked box alone does not establish that all conditions are met; absent a verified process, no blanket loss of the right should be asserted.

After valid unwinding of the contract, the contractual right of use ends. The customer should remove the software from affected devices. The service may block further activations; already issued offline licence files cannot reliably be remotely disabled. No remote instant shutdown is promised.

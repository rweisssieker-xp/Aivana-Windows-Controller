# MAKRIVO – withdrawal notice and declaration aid (draft)

**Review draft, 15 November 2026: unpublished and not legally approved.** This English text describes the German consumer scenario for a contract for digital content not supplied on a tangible medium. It is not a determination of the applicable law and does not establish that English is sufficient for sales elsewhere. The actual purchase flow, electronic withdrawal function, statutory form, contract confirmation and every market's requirements need review before use.

## Withdrawal notice – proposed customer wording

### Your right to withdraw

You may withdraw from this contract within 14 days without giving a reason. The period is 14 days from the day the contract is concluded. The statutory requirements for informing you about your right of withdrawal also apply to the start of the period.

To exercise your right, inform us of your decision by an unequivocal statement, for example by letter or email:

Aivana GmbH, Paulusstr. 45a – Hinterhaus – LOFT45, 33602 Bielefeld, Germany. Email: info@aivana-gmbh.ai. Telephone: +49 521 92278996.

You may use the statutory model withdrawal form, but you do not have to. To meet the deadline, it is enough to send your declaration before the withdrawal period expires.

### Effects of withdrawal

If you validly withdraw from this contract, we will reimburse payments received from you no later than 14 days after the day we receive your withdrawal declaration. We will use the same payment method as for the original payment unless we expressly agree otherwise with you and doing so does not incur costs for you. We will not charge you a fee for the reimbursement.

After valid unwinding, the contractual right of use ends. You should remove the software from the affected PCs. Already issued offline licence files cannot reliably be disabled remotely; further activations may be blocked.

### Early performance for digital content

An existing right of withdrawal for paid digital content not supplied on a tangible medium can expire early if the applicable legal conditions are met. These include your express consent to performance before the withdrawal period ends, your acknowledgement that the right is lost when performance begins, our provision of the required contract confirmation, and the actual start of performance. General acceptance of purchase terms alone does not replace these conditions.

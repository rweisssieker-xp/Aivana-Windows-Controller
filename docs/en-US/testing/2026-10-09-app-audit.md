## Reproduced and fixed

- A focused action selector prevented its new fields from rendering. The original focused Volume → Set regression failed before the correction. Structural selections now force rendering, while ordinary background refresh preserves editing.
- Switching an action kind and returning discarded launch arguments, working directories and audio device selections. The editor now caches the complete step per kind.
- Background refresh could recreate a focused password editor or a pending OBS setup operation. Password/slider focus and outstanding setup work now preserve the editor.
- Draft validation omitted empty macros, OBS parameters, Corsair binding validity/duplicates and the reserved palette hotkey. Draft checks now share the core validators and check profile conflicts before saving/testing.
- Pause/Cancel All could miss an execution waiting for target-window restoration. A captured cancellation generation now covers restoration and dispatch, including pause followed by resume, import and disposal.
